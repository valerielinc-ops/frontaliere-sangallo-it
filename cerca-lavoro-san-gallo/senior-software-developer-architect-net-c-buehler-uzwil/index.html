Descrizione completa

Bühler's strategic JointForces program is shaping the future user interface and software platform for more than 100 machine types used in the production of chocolate, flour, coffee, animal feed, cornflakes, paints, and many other products. To help turn this vision into a scalable global platform, we are looking for a Senior Software Developer/Architect .NET/C#. In this key role, you will combine hands-on technical leadership with architectural responsibility, guide two development teams, and drive the continuous evolution and adoption of our machine software platform across the Bühler Group.

Aufgaben

  • You take technical leadership for the implementation of Bühler's machine software platform together with two development teams
  • You share system architecture responsibilities with your peer and contribute to the platform's architectural direction, with the opportunity to develop further into a System Architect role
  • You drive the continuous development of the group-wide platform using .NET/C#, Angular, and TypeScript, ensuring scalable and maintainable solutions
  • You help establish the platform across the organization by building a strong developer community and introducing the required processes, standards, and systems
  • You support Product Owners in defining technical enablers and ensure that maintenance and lifecycle topics are reflected in the backlog based on business needs

Anforderungen

  • Degree from a university, university of applied sciences, or higher technical college in Software Engineering, Computer Science, or a comparable field
  • Several years of experience as a Senior Software Developer/Architect, or in a comparable senior technical role, ideally with strong expertise in .NET/C#; Angular and TypeScript would be a plus
  • Proven ability to communicate complex technical, architectural, and organizational topics clearly and concisely to developers, Product Owners, and other stakeholders
  • Collaborative and pragmatic team player with the ability to build strong relationships, influence without direct authority, and mobilize people around a shared technical vision
  • Experience working in international and hybrid development environments, combined with good English skills
  • German is an advantage
